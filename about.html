Meet Our Founder & CEO

Hi, I'm Franchesca.

Franchesca Bethel doesn't build one brand — she builds ecosystems. A marketing strategist, brand builder, and multi-venture founder based in Jacksonville, Florida, Franchesca holds a BS in Fashion Merchandising and Retailing and an MBA in Operations and Supply Chain Management — pairing an eye for aesthetics with the operational rigor to actually scale it.

With five years of e-commerce experience, she pairs sharp performance analysis and SEO instincts with an unmistakably elevated, feminine aesthetic. Marketing Maison is her agency — giving creatives and event hosts the marketing and PR firepower usually reserved for brands with bigger budgets.

Our Philosophy

Three words. One way of working. No exceptions.

01

Own It.

Your marketing should belong to you — not to a platform that can change its algorithm tomorrow. We prioritize owned channels: email lists, SMS, referral programs, and direct marketing that you control completely. Social media is a tool, not a strategy. When you rent your audience from Instagram or TikTok, you're one policy change away from losing everything you built. We build the foundation on ground you actually own.

02

Build It.

Every recommendation we make is rooted in real data — your actual analytics, your real competitors, your industry's actual benchmarks, and your specific business goals. We don't fabricate metrics or chase vanity numbers. Before we build anything, we understand where you actually stand. Marketing built on honest data compounds. Marketing built on made-up numbers collapses.

03

Scale It.

Everything we do is organic — no paid ads, no shortcuts. We build KPI-based systems that are monitored, reviewable, and tied directly to your business goals. You'll always know what's working, why it's working, and what comes next. Real growth is systematic, not accidental. We scale what can be measured, and we measure everything.

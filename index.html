The Philosophy

Owned channels compound. Rented channels evict you.

Own It

Email · SMS · Website · SEO · Referral

  • You control the audience, the data, and the cadence.
  • Compounds month over month with no platform tax.
  • Survives algorithm changes, ad cost spikes, and bans.
  • Becomes a transferable asset on your balance sheet.

Rent It

Instagram · TikTok · Paid Social · Influencer

  • You're a tenant. Rent goes up. Eviction is a policy change.
  • Reach is metered, capped, and re-priced quarterly.
  • Audience access can be revoked overnight.
  • Useful — but only as a feeder for what you own.

Common Questions

The diagnosis is a paid, two-week audit of your current marketing infrastructure — what you have, what's leaking, and what to build first. It costs $1,500 and produces a written report with a prioritized fix order. That fee is applied to the first month of any ongoing engagement. We start every client relationship this way because a $1,500 honest report is better for everyone than a $5,000/month engagement built on guesswork.

Yes. We're based in Jacksonville but work with clients across the US. The diagnosis and ongoing work are fully remote. All working sessions are video calls; deliverables are shared asynchronously through a shared workspace.

Owned channels compound — meaning results build over time rather than appearing overnight. Most clients see their first measurable revenue attribution from a new owned channel within 60–90 days of launch. SEO typically compounds over 6–12 months. We're explicit about this timeline with every client and build toward it deliberately.

Both founders run every diagnosis. Senior strategists run every ongoing engagement. We don't use offshore labor or junior account managers as your day-to-day contact. We're intentionally small — we'd rather serve fewer clients well than scale a commodity service.

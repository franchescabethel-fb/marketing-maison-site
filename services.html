#04

Email & SMS Marketing

Your highest-ROI marketing channel — built on a list you own outright, not an algorithm you rent.

Start with a discovery call

What It Means

Email and SMS give you a direct line to your audience that no platform can take away. We build full acquisition and retention systems: welcome sequences that convert new subscribers into buyers, nurture flows that keep existing customers engaged, and re-engagement campaigns that win back the ones who've gone quiet.

Our Approach

We start with your subscriber list health and deliverability audit. From there we map out your full customer lifecycle and identify the automations that will have the biggest impact first. We write and design each sequence, set up the technical infrastructure (including DKIM, SPF, and DMARC for deliverability), and continuously A/B test subject lines, send times, and CTAs to maximize open and click rates.

What You Get

  • List health + deliverability audit
  • Welcome & onboarding sequence
  • Nurture email flows
  • Promotional campaign templates
  • SMS opt-in setup + sequences
  • A/B testing program
  • Win-back campaigns
  • Monthly performance reporting

Start with a discovery call

What It Means
